Electrical Upgrades

Upgrade lighting, panels, breakers, and more for better efficiency.

About the service:

Enhance the safety and efficiency of your electrical system with our electrical upgrade services. Whether you need LED lighting, a new panel box, or updated breakers, we provide expert solutions to meet your needs. We also offer replacement of service equipment to provide more amperage and replace old breaker or fuse boxes. Upgrading your electrical system not only boosts performance but also ensures long-term reliability and energy savings.

What’s Included?

  • Consultation to assess upgrade needs
  • Replacement of service equipment to provide more amperage
  • Replacement of old breaker or fuse boxes
  • Installation of LED lighting, panel boxes, and breakers
  • Improved wiring and electrical infrastructure for efficiency
  • Code-compliant work to meet safety standards
